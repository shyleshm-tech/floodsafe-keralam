Scoring Rules

Distance: shorter road distance = higher score.
Flooding: Low=1, Medium=0.5, High=0.
Traffic: Low=1, Medium=0.5, High=0.
Road Condition: Good=1, Fair=0.5, Poor=0.
Road Width: Wide=1, Medium=0.5, Narrow=0.
Road Blockages: None=1, Minor=0.5, Major=0.
Hospital Accessibility: Direct=1, Moderate=0.5, Indirect=0.
